Operations handbook (sample text for cache tests, CC0)

Section 1: Rack power and cooling
Rule 1.1: Operators log the reason for every rack power and cooling action in the shared ticket before and after the work, name the owner, and attach the reading they used. If two readings disagree by more than five percent, the lower-risk action is taken first and the gap is noted for review.
