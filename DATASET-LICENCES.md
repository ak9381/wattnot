## Meta's terms for model outputs

Sources, read on 4 Nov 2026:
- [Meta Model API Terms of Service](https://dev.meta.ai/legal/terms-of-service) (last updated 9 Oct 2026)
- [Help Center: will Meta claim copyright over outputs?](https://dev.meta.ai/help/policies-and-privacy/output-copyright)
- [Muse Glimmer 30B model card](https://huggingface.co/meta-models/Muse-Glimmer-30B), [licence](https://huggingface.co/meta-models/Muse-Glimmer-30B/blob/main/LICENSE) and [usage policy](https://huggingface.co/meta-models/Muse-Glimmer-30B/blob/c4808aaa0214dfe739f9074ac0054c77a42933cd/USAGE_POLICY.md)

What they say:
- **API outputs are ours.** Section 4.1: "you own all rights, title, and interests in and to your Inputs and Outputs, ... and Meta does not claim any ownership". This covers Muse Image images, the Spark-made tasks, and Spark, Spark Open, Wonder, SAM and Voice Transcribe answers. Spark Open on Baseten is a hosted platform (Exhibit C), and the same terms still govern it.
- **Glimmer answers** come from the self-hosted open weights. Those are Apache 2.0 with the Glimmer usage policy. Apache doesn't restrict outputs, so the answers are ours too.
- **Limits that still apply when we publish:**
  - 10.1(ix): nobody may use the outputs to train a model that competes with Meta's. Evaluation and benchmarking of your own system is allowed. We state this in the dataset card.
  - 10.1(xi): no public benchmarks of Meta's services to market a competing product. WattNot runs on Meta's models and doesn't compete with them, so our numbers are fine.
  - Section 1: where the law requires it, label AI-generated content. The sets already say "generated by Muse Image" / "Spark-generated" / SYNTHETIC.
  - Section 7: we get no trademark licence. Name the models factually, with no Meta logos as endorsement.
  - Outputs "may include materials subject to third-party licences" (4.1). Our outputs are synthetic gauges, racks, charts and short answers, so we don't expect any.

**Verdict:** Muse Image images, Spark-made tasks and model answers can be published. We release them as CC0 (images and tasks) and CC BY 4.0 (results). The card must carry the "no training of competing models" line and the AI-generated labels.

## Results and receipts

Run outputs (`replays/`, `receipts/`, `data/*` run folders) are ours, released under CC BY 4.0 (`replays/LICENSE-DATA.md`, `receipts/LICENSE-DATA.md`). Model answers inside them are Meta model outputs; see Meta's terms above.

## Decisions (Akash, 4 Nov 2026)

1. **Voice:** keep the test cases, re-voiced with Piper. The edge-tts audio is dropped.
2. **Meta outputs:** checked against Meta's public terms. They can be published (see above).
3. **Excluded from the public set:** `rack/rack.jpg` and `rack/ai/rack.jpg`. They stay in the repo for the demo but are not in any data release.
4. **Licences:** CC0 for our prompt sets, CC BY 4.0 for results and receipts.
5. **Share-alike still applies:** anything drawn on the CC BY-SA photos (SAM masks, audit overlays) ships under CC BY-SA, not CC0.
