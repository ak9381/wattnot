The idea

A saving is only a claim
until someone else can check it.

So every change WattNot makes ends in a signed receipt. The receipt holds the before and after numbers, the quality check, and a fingerprint. Change one digit and the check fails. Try it on a real sample below.

Power on demand

 
When the grid asks,
the power comes down.

 
When the grid operator asks a data centre to use less, WattNot lowers the GPU power limit step by step and watches latency and answer quality all the way. Every step goes into a log, and the receipt covers the whole event.

 

 

 
All of this is a simulated GPU. A real run on rented hardware is the next step, and it will be labelled MEASURED only if it is. Open the full chart →






 
You stay in control

 
Nothing changes
until you say so.

 
WattNot tests each change on your own prompts before it touches anything. You see what it did to power, speed and answer quality. Then you approve it, or you leave things as they are. If you change your mind later, one step puts the old setting back.
