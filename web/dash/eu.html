Why it matters

Energy Efficiency Directive

Data centres with ≥500 kW installed IT power must report energy use, water input, waste-heat reuse, renewables and related KPIs to the EU database every year (by 15 May, for the previous year). PUE and WUE are calculated from these.

AI Act · general-purpose models

Providers of general-purpose AI models must keep technical documentation that includes the model's known or estimated energy consumption (Art. 53(1)(a), Annex XI).

The gap

Facility meters give kWh for the building, not per answer. Nobody can say what one useful answer cost.

How WattNot helps

Water per answer

How water is worked out: measured energy (kWh) × the site's water use per kWh (WUE). Water: 0.36 L/kWh (on-site cooling, ESTIMATE); up to 1.8 L/kWh including power-plant water (ESTIMATE).
